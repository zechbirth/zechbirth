## About Me

I am a **Senior Systems Analyst and Technology Consultant** working across business, technology and people to design and evolve digital solutions for complex corporate environments.

My experience combines **SAP, HR technology, enterprise integrations, solution architecture, automation, governance, cybersecurity and Responsible AI**.

I contribute throughout the solution lifecycle:

```text
Problem discovery
      ↓
Requirements and impact analysis
      ↓
Solution and integration design
      ↓
Technical validation and risk assessment
      ↓
Testing and implementation
      ↓
Operational monitoring and continuous improvement
```

My goal is to transform complex business needs into solutions that are **clear, secure, scalable, traceable and reliable**.
